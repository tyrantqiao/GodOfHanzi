## 1. 一刀断流（斩断）

- 字义关联：斩字本义，一刀两断；对应刀「引爆灼痕」的终结一击。
- 视觉主体：悬瀑中段被一道斜向刀势分开，水帘裂出空隙，断口处飞白留痕。
- 构图：瀑布纵贯左右两缘，刀势自右上至左下横切画面中央，水雾即天然留白；不画持刀者。
- 提示词：

  A great cliff waterfall split mid-fall by a single diagonal blade arc, the water parted into two curving sheets with a clear gap along the cut, the cut line drawn as a pale feibai dry-brush stroke with ink-green blade tint, mist bursting at the divide, dark cliff textures framing top and bottom. Pure force-of-severance image, the invisible blade felt only by its cut.

- 推荐文件名：`martial-cleftfall.png`

## 2. 锐试寒冰（破势）

- 字义关联：以刃试冰、裂纹破势；对应破开敌人蓄积之势。
- 视觉主体：黎明冻结的江面，一记斜斩让整片薄冰炸起放射裂纹，刃影掠出画面左缘。
- 构图：冰面纹理铺满全幅，裂纹以一个偏左下命中点放射，右上留天光薄雾；刀形只在左缘露出一段刀脊飞白。
- 提示词：

  A frozen river at grey dawn, one diagonal sabre cut detonating a radial fan of cracks across thin ice, the blade itself only suggested by a short ink-green spine stroke exiting the left edge with feibai trailing, shards of pale ice lifting along the crack lines, cold silver-blue dawn sky melting into central mist. Breaking-momentum moment caught one heartbeat after impact.

- 推荐文件名：`martial-icecut.png`

## 3. 孤刀如铁（护身）

- 字义关联：刀立如人守，不动之护；对应刀「护身」的字义分支。
- 视觉主体：山口逆风中，一柄长刀倒插于石缝，刀身缠布柄与褪色朱砂缨被风扯直，远寇影踟蹰。
- 构图：刀为纵向中轴偏左，逆风斜坡压向右下，远处贼寇剪影小而模糊置于右上山雾；中心刀身之后的雾带留白。
- 提示词：

  A lone long dao saber planted upside down in a rocky mountain gap, cloth-wrapped hilt and a faded cinnabar tassel pulled straight by headwind, wind-blown grass bending away, distant small bandit silhouettes hesitating in upper mist beyond an invisible line. The blade itself is the ward, immovable resolve, no fighter drawn, no gore.

- 推荐文件名：`martial-wardblade.png`

## 4. 案前落刃（决断）

- 字义关联：决于口者速，决于手者断；对应「决断」情绪而非伤害对象。
- 视觉主体：灯下案头，一卷摊开的竹简与半局残棋之上，一只刀鞘横放、刀出半寸，寒光一线。
- 构图：案面斜向透视占下三分之二，刀半出鞘指向右上，灯晕收在左上；中心留白为案后素墙。
- 提示词：

  Night desk scene under a small oil lamp: an unrolled bamboo slip and half-finished go board, across them a dao saber lying in its lacquered scabbard drawn open just one inch, a thin pale line of edge light in that gap, decision-weight of a held breath, warm ivory wall of the room left empty at middle, dark timber shadows. Still-life tension, no person, no blood.

- 推荐文件名：`martial-halfdraw.png`

## 5. 飞白十步（连击）

- 字义关联：刀势相接、连斩不断；对应刀卡连招与笔势相接的制卡记忆。
- 视觉主体：雨竹间一道刀光连斩十步，十道长短不一的飞白刀痕在竹幕上叠加成节奏。
- 构图：竹竿纵向排列被斜向刀痕连续截出短促断口，视觉动线自左上向右下阶梯推进；雨丝淡灰，中心两竿竹后的雾带留白。
- 提示词：

  Driving rain in a dense bamboo grove pierced by a stepping blade combination, ten overlapping diagonal feibai cut marks of varying length scored across the scene like a rhythmic brush sequence, several bamboo trunks severed mid-height springing apart at the cuts, rain slants in pale grey ink, deep jade foliage darkening to edges. Combo expressed as calligraphic strike rhythm on the grove itself.

- 推荐文件名：`martial-stepcuts.png`

## 6. 刀收寒潭（收势）

- 字义关联：敛锋归鞘、动静相生；对应一式收势与战后余静。
- 视觉主体：夜尽的寒潭边，一人影远小正还刀入鞘，潭面最后一道刃痕涟漪缓缓平复。
- 构图：潭面占下三分之二，倒映将明的天色，还刀者置于右下极小剪影，一道细亮涟漪弧线划过中心。
- 提示词：

  A still cold pool at night's end beside dark stones, a tiny distant cloaked figure sheathing a dao saber at the lower right rim, the pool surface holding one last thin bright ripple arc like a fading cut line, inverted dusk sky across the water, pale mist band over the centre of the pool. Quiet aftermath, restraint after violence, calm composed mood, no gore, face not visible.

- 推荐文件名：`martial-sheathpool.png`
