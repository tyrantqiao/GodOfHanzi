## 1. 雪夜初燃（点燃）

- 字义关联：火之初起，一点即燃；对应「先火」教学的第一下点燃。
- 视觉主体：雪夜茅檐下，火镰敲出的一点火星正落入干柴窝，柴堆芯已透出第一缕朱砂焰形。
- 构图：檐角与垂雪占上部，柴堆位于下三分之一偏左，火星到焰芯形成斜向递进动势；中部雪雾留白。
- 提示词（拼接共用框架后追加）：

  A single spark just struck from a steel fire striker falls onto a bundle of dry brushwood under a snow-laden thatched eave, the kindling heart catching its first small cinnabar flame shape, deep blue-grey snow dusk around, warm ochre light pooling only near the kindling, tiny snow dots low density. Intimate beginning-of-fire moment.

- 推荐文件名：`fire-firstspark.png`

## 2. 灯脉传夜（延续）

- 字义关联：薪尽火传、灯灯相续；火「可反复抽到、持续供能」的画意表达。
- 视觉主体：山涧栈道上悬成一串的旧灯笼，最近一盏正从前一盏倾身借火，光点向画面深处延伸。
- 构图：灯笼队列自右下向左上对角排布，远近尺度递减表现延续不绝；中部山雾留白；人物仅允许远小剪影且不可为视觉主体。
- 提示词：

  A row of old paper lanterns hung along a mountain plank path at night, the nearest lantern tilted receiving flame from its neighbour, a chain of warm cinnabar-ochre light points receding into distant mist, dark pine silhouettes, faint reflected stream below. Rhythm of passing flame, quiet perseverance mood.

- 推荐文件名：`fire-lanternchain.png`

## 3. 劫灰凝珠（凝聚）

- 字义关联：万焰归一、凝火成髓；对应灼痕在目标身上蓄积的意象。
- 视觉主体：冷灰炭堆中心，一缕朱砂火舌盘旋收束成一颗将凝未凝的火珠。
- 构图：炭堆铺满下部，螺旋火舌将视线收向画面中心偏上；四周压暗灰以衬托中心小火；火珠不大，光不外溢。
- 提示词：

  A cold grey ash and charcoal mound filling the lower frame, at its centre a single cinnabar flame tongue spiralling inward and condensing into a small ember pearl no larger than a fist, dense ink-grey surroundings, restrained ochre rim light only around the spiral core. Contained power, coalescence mood, NOT an explosion.

- 推荐文件名：`fire-emberpearl.png`

## 4. 原燎势成（爆发）

- 字义关联：星火成燎、势不可收；对应引爆灼痕的爆发动作。
- 视觉主体：北坡荒原上一线火线借风张开，断墨笔势的焰尾扫成弧形扇面，远处山脊压顶。
- 构图：火线沿下三分之一横贯，右端炸开为主焰形，飞白断纹表现速度；中部烟雾留白；不画受火对象、不见血腥。
- 提示词：

  A prairie fire line racing along a dry northern hillside at dusk, the right flank bursting into a wide fan of curved cinnabar flame shapes with broken charcoal edges and feibai dry-brush gaps, thin smoke ribbons rising into an ink-washed mountain ridge above, scattered charred strokes in the burnt wake. Explosive momentum expressed by brush rhythm, NOT glowing particles.

- 推荐文件名：`fire-prairie.png`

## 5. 炉火守窗（守护）

- 字义关联：火为御寒之守；对应火在寒冬纪元的庇护含义。
- 视觉主体：风雪夜窗内一炉旺火，暖焰形撑起半个窗格；窗外霜青寒气被逼退成淡影。
- 构图：以窗棂分内外：内侧炉火与米纸暖光，外侧灰青风雪；中心留白置于窗内炉前空塌处。
- 提示词：

  View through a timber window frame on a stormy winter night: inside, a bronze stove with steady layered cinnabar flame shapes warming an empty woven mat and paper-backed walls in warm ivory, outside, pale grey-teal snow gusts driven against the sill, frost threads on the outer glass edge only. Shelter and vigil mood, strong warm-cool split along the window, no person inside frame.

- 推荐文件名：`fire-hearthwatch.png`

## 6. 石室传焰（传承）

- 字义关联：长明灯脉、师传徒承；对应导师赠卡与入门叙事。
- 视觉主体：古石室深处，一支长明巨烛正被一柄新烛分引火种，两条焰形一小一大相望。
- 构图：巨烛居左、新烛居右下方，火种相递的弧线为视觉动线；壁面抽象凿痕作底纹；中部石雾留白。
- 提示词：

  Deep inside an old cut-stone chamber, a towering ancient candle burning low shares its flame downward to a fresh slim candle held close, two cinnabar flame shapes leaning toward each other across a thin arc of transferred fire, aged soot patterns and abstract chisel marks on the walls, shafts of pale light from a roof oculus. Lineage and passing-the-torch solemnity, monumental quiet scale. No readable wall carvings.

- 推荐文件名：`fire-candletorch.png`
