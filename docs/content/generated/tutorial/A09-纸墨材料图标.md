## 1. 薄纸

- 外观设定：三张错开的米白薄片，边缘毛裁带纤维丝，最上一张右角微微翘起透光；厚度感靠层间 1px 灰影表达。
- 颜色：纸 `#f4eedf`/`#eee8db`，纤维丝与投影 `#718078` 低透明。
- 材料描述（≤20字）：质薄受墨即透，落笔贵爽利，宜书火字。
- SVG 要点：三片错位 4px 的圆角矩形 path，顶片右上角二次贝塞尔上翘；边缘用 dasharray 1 2 细线模拟毛边。
- 位图提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game material icon, Chinese ink wash style. A small stack of three thin rice-paper sheets, deckled fibrous edges, top-right corner slightly lifted catching pale light, warm ivory paper tones on transparent ground, minimal grey-teal shadow lines. No text, no numbers. Genuine transparent background.

## 2. 韧纸

- 外观设定：两张厚纸对叠，纸面可见横向帘纹与零星长纤维，边角挺括不卷，厚度为薄纸两倍；一侧压一枚粗麻镇纸角。
- 颜色：纸 `#eee8db` 偏旧、帘纹 `#d7cdb9`，镇纸角灰青 `#597b79`。
- 材料描述（≤20字）：厚帘密纹，可经重写，墨浮于面。
- SVG 要点：矩形加 3px 圆角与两条内斜切角表现挺括；帘纹用 4 条 0.8px 水平线；长纤维 2 根断续曲线。
- 位图提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game material icon, Chinese ink wash style. Two sheets of thick textured Xuan paper stacked, visible laid-lines and long paper fibres, crisp unsticking corners, a small grey-teal linen-covered weight pressing one corner, aged ivory tones. Sturdier and thicker than the thin-paper icon. No text, no numbers. Genuine transparent background.

## 3. 朱砂墨

- 外观设定：一枚赭红墨碇（长方倒角），正面磨出一面凹弧露出更深朱色，碇侧一点未干的墨光；旁依半截朱砂条。
- 颜色：墨体 `#98483c`，凹弧暗部 `#6e352c`，墨光留白纸白；朱砂条颗粒感用 3 点深赭。
- 材料描述（≤20字）：朱砂入胶，色烈纹显，宜书火字。
- 区分逻辑：与松烟墨同形同角度，仅以色与表面质感区分，保证缩略时成对可辨。
- 位图提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game material icon, Chinese ink wash style. A rectangular cinnabar-red ink cake with beveled edges, its ground surface a shallow darker arc with one wet sheen highlight, a small stub of raw cinnabar stick leaning beside it, muted brick-red mineral tones. No text, no numbers. Genuine transparent background.

## 4. 松烟墨

- 外观设定：与朱砂墨同形墨碇，通体松黑泛青，磨面哑光无釉色，侧靠一枚带松针屑的墨盒盖。
- 颜色：墨体 `#293b32`，磨面 `#1f2d26`，松针屑灰青点。
- 材料描述（≤20字）：松烟凝黑，墨沉骨清，宜书刀字。
- 位图提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game material icon, Chinese ink wash style. The same rectangular ink cake shape as the cinnabar set but in deep pine-soot black with a faint green-ink undertone, matte ground surface without sheen, a small ink-stone lid with scattered pine-needle crumbs leaning beside it. No text, no numbers. Genuine transparent background.
