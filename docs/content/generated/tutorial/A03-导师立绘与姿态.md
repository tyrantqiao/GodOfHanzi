## 1. 递卡 offering（递出字卡）

- 动作说明：上身微前倾，右手自胸前双手托出两张叠放的素色字卡递向画面右前方，杖斜夹于右臂弯；左手虚扶卡角，目光低垂落于卡上，神情郑重而温和。
- 提示词：

  Pose: leaning slightly forward, both hands presenting two plain cream paper cards stacked and fanned at chest height toward the front-right, the gnarled staff tucked against his right forearm, eyes lowered onto the cards with grave gentleness, sleeves draping forward. Cards are blank paper, no glyph, no seal marks.

- 提示词：

  Pose: standing firm as a ward, staff planted forward in his right hand, left hand raised to chest height in a two-finger spell gesture toward the right, beard and sleeve edges lifted by a quiet outward gust toward frame-left, gaze level and watchful over the shoulder of an unseen disciple behind him at frame-left. Authority without aggression, NO energy circles, NO glowing runes.

## 3. 指点 pointing（指点书写）

- 动作说明：侧身向左下俯就（面向书写的弟子），右手食指前下方点出，指间虚捏一管细笔作示范，左手背于身后持杖横搁臂弯；头微侧，眉眼专注，如隔案讲解笔画。
- 方向说明：此姿态面向画面左下，对应笔案在主角一侧的构图；与其余三图朝向不同，接入时按 UI 站位镜像处理。
- 提示词：

  Pose: turned toward the lower left, upper body bending slightly over an unseen writing desk, right index finger extended pointing down-forward as if tracing a stroke in the air, slender brush held loosely between the same fingers, left hand behind his back resting on the horizontal staff, head tilted, brows raised in patient explanation. No desk drawn, pointing gesture dominant.

## 4. 欣慰 pleased（认可入门）

- 动作说明：收势直立，双手交叠拄杖，杖横置身前，下颌微抬望向右侧远处的弟子，眼角与须梢舒展，唇角一线极浅的笑意；袍袖垂定，仪态松弛而不失庄重。
- 提示词：

  Pose: upright at ease, both hands folded atop the horizontal staff before him, chin lifted gazing right toward the departed apprentice, eyes softened with faint proud smile lines, beard settled calm, sleeves hanging still. Quiet satisfied approval expressed through posture and eyes only, restrained, no broad smile, no tears.
