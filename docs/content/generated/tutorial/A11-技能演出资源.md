## 火（朱砂断墨）

### 1. 凝字蓄势（建议 250ms）

- 视觉描述：主角卡手前方，数缕炭墨色断墨弧绕一个纸白空腔缓缓旋聚，弧端渐次染上朱砂，火形未成、势先收拢。
- 素材：`fx-fire-gather.svg`（3 层断墨弧，CSS 旋转，无需位图）。
- 提示词（位图备选）：

  Production transparent PNG game VFX frame, Chinese ink wash style. Several broken charcoal-black ink arcs spiralling around an empty paper-white core in front of a caster's hand area, arc tips just starting to tint cinnabar-red (#98483c), motion conveyed by dry-brush gaps along each arc. Small compact scale, NO flames yet, NO glow ball. No character drawn. Genuine transparent background.

### 2. 字义成物（建议 300ms）

- 视觉描述：旋聚的断墨弧一步合为一支弯曲朱砂焰形——炭墨外缘、赭红焰身、一点暖纸焰心，焰形离手向右探出，尾部拖两粒断墨火星。
- 素材：`fx-fire-form.png`（单帧主体，2x）；焰形轮廓与 `cinnabar-flame.svg` 同语言，可直接矢量化。
- 提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game VFX sprite, Chinese ink wash style. One curved stylized cinnabar flame shape taking form, charcoal-black outer edge, ochre-red body, tiny warm-paper core highlight, two detached dry-brush ember dashes trailing behind its base. Flame reads as brush-drawn calligraphy stroke, NOT realistic fire, NO neon, NO bloom. Facing right. Genuine transparent background.

### 3. 命中（建议 200ms + 敌人受击帧同步）

- 视觉描述：焰形扑至敌人身上摊成环状断痕爆燃，爆点外圈是散墨断痕而非圆环，两三点朱砂灼痕屑嵌入敌人轮廓边缘（灼痕标记本体仍由界面 `#enemy-states` 文字承担）。
- 素材：`fx-fire-strike.png`；可选 `fx-fire-strike-b.svg`（断痕环矢量化）。
- 提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game VFX frame, Chinese ink wash style. A flame impact splayed against an invisible vertical surface, center burst of ochre-red broken ink strokes arranged as a shattered ring, sparse cinnabar flecks scattering outward-right, charred black roots at the burst center. Impact expressed by calligraphic fracture, NO fireball, NO smoke plume. Genuine transparent background.

### 4. 生效余墨消散（建议 350ms）

- 视觉描述：断痕环自外向内褪为灰青冷墨，残余焰形缩成一缕细烟向左上散入雪林；地面留两点将熄的赭色墨渍，0.5s 后淡出。
- 素材：`fx-fire-fade.svg`（褪变用 CSS filter/opacity 序列，不逐帧）。
- 提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game VFX frame, Chinese ink wash style. Fading aftermath of an ink-flame burst: broken ring strokes drained to cold grey-teal (#597b79) dry brush, one thin smoke ribbon curling up-left, two small dying ochre ink stains near the ground line. Quiet extinction mood, NO embers rising, NO light. Genuine transparent background.

## 刀（墨青刀骨、飞白刀痕）

### 1. 凝字蓄势（建议 250ms）

- 视觉描述：主角身前一线墨青刀骨虚影竖立成形，如淡墨起稿：刀柄缠布、短护手、微弯刀身轮廓以断笔线一圈圈收拢。
- 素材：`fx-blade-gather.svg`（淡影轮廓 + dasharray 收拢动画）。
- 提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game VFX frame, Chinese ink wash style. A ghost outline of a single Chinese dao saber forming from converging broken ink-green (#293b32) sketch strokes — wrapped handle, short guard, gently curved blade drawn as if under construction, dry-brush gaps along the outline. Flat pale tint, NO steel shine, NO solid blade yet. Facing right, blade tip up. Genuine transparent background.

### 2. 字义成物（建议 300ms）

- 视觉描述：虚影一笔落定成实体墨刀：墨青刀骨、浅纸刀面、刃口一线留白，刀身微颤甩出两滴松烟墨点；沿用 `summoned-blade.svg` 终形，只补飞白刃纹。
- 素材：`fx-blade-form.png`（或由 `summoned-blade.svg` 加 CSS 描边动画平替，优先矢量）。
- 提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game VFX sprite, Chinese ink wash style. A solid ink-styled Chinese dao saber just materialized: dark ink-green spine, pale paper-toned blade face with feibai dry-brush grain along it, a reserved-white edge line, two tiny pine-soot ink droplets flung from the tip, blade angled for a rightward downward cut. NO chrome gradient, NO gold ornaments, NO glow. Genuine transparent background.

### 3. 命中（建议 180ms，全四段最短）

- 视觉描述：一道斜向飞白刀痕自右上劈至左下，痕末在敌人位置绽开 2–3 根短断墨；刀体本身不滞留（与现「实体墨刀掠过」差异：刀痕即一切）。
- 素材：`fx-blade-strike.svg`（单条飞白 path + 断点组）。
- 提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game VFX frame, Chinese ink wash style. One bold diagonal feibai slash mark from upper-right to lower-left, the stroke breaking into dry gaps along its length, 2–3 short ink-green fracture ticks bursting at the impact end. The slash itself is the whole effect, NO saber visible, NO light trail. Genuine transparent background.

### 4. 生效余墨消散（建议 350ms）

- 视觉描述：飞白刀痕自中段断开成两截，坠为几点墨渍沿假想地面积聚，随即被雪色盖淡；敌人身上不留任何附着物（灼痕归 A12）。
- 素材：`fx-blade-fade.svg`。
- 提示词：

  Production transparent PNG game VFX frame, Chinese ink wash style. A fading slash mark splitting mid-way into two drooping dry-brush segments that fall as small ink-green stains settling on an implied ground line, snow-pale wash beginning to cover the stains. Silence after the cut, NO dripping, NO mist. Genuine transparent background.

## 组合与工程备注

- 四段总时长建议 火 1.1s / 刀 1.08s，均 ≤ 现 `busy` 锁 500ms + 渲染节奏的两倍以内；接入时若与结算动画冲突，优先压缩「消散」段。
- 演出层 z-order：背景 < 立绘 < 特效 < 伤害数字/状态文字 < 界面。任何素材不得含文字与数字。
- 静态替代帧（减少动态偏好）：各段第 2 素材直接静置 400ms 顺序显示即可，无需新文件。
