## 1. 持卡 holdcard（手牌阶段默认）

- 动作说明：自然站立略沉肩，右手屈臂将一张素色纸卡举至胸前端详，卡面朝向自己（观众只见卡背与手指），左手持巨笔斜垂体侧；视线落卡，微颔首。
- 与现有 `shen-yan-ready-v2.png` 区分：ready 是伸手向外递卡备战，持卡是收卡审视，肘部内收、卡不越肩线。
- 提示词（拼接身份块后追加）：

  Pose: calmly standing, right arm folded inward holding a small blank cream paper card at chest height viewing it, card back toward viewer, left hand holding the giant brush slanting down beside him, weight even on both feet, chin slightly lowered toward the card, focused quiet expression, facing right. Card is plain paper only, no glyph, no markings.

## 2. 出牌 playcard（施法瞬间）

- 动作说明：侧身弓步朝右，右手自腰间向前挥出，两指夹卡甩向画面右缘，卡已离指带一线笔势余韵；左臂后摆持笔平衡，围巾与袍裾被动作拉出水平飞白。
- 提示词：

  Pose: forward lunging stance facing right, right arm thrust forward releasing a small paper card from two fingers, the card just leaving the hand with one trailing dry-brush motion stroke, left arm swept back holding the giant brush for balance, vermilion scarf and robe hems pulled into horizontal feibai streaks by the motion, eyes locked forward, decisive expression. Motion expressed only by brush strokes, no flames, no light trails.

## 3. 受击 hit（敌方回合命中）

- 动作说明：重心骤撤向右后方，后手撑地、前臂抬挡面部，巨笔横拄身前代挡；上身扭转，靴尖不离基线，发带与围巾向左前飘（受击方向来自右，即敌人侧）。
- 方向核对：敌人朝左、主角朝右，受击冲量应把衣饰吹向画面左侧。
- 提示词：

  Pose: sudden recoil, torso wrenched backward-right while feet stay near the baseline, left forearm raised shielding the face, right hand driving the giant brush horizontal into the incoming line from the right as a makeshift guard, scarf and sleeve edges whipped toward frame-left by the impact gust, teeth lightly gritted, eyes squeezed half shut. Defensive pain, not exaggerated agony, no blood, no wound marks.

## 4. 制卡 crafting（笔案侧影）

- 动作说明：单膝跪坐案前，上身前倾，右手悬腕执竹管小笔作势欲落（案上砚墨与纸可见），巨笔收靠案侧；左袖撩起，围巾垂落肩前；神情专注宁静，朝向左前方案几（此动作允许朝左，非战斗朝向）。
- 提示词：

  Pose: kneeling at a low writing desk shown in profile facing left, upper body leaning forward, right hand hovering wrist raised holding a slim writing brush about to descend onto paper, giant brush rested against the desk side, left sleeve tucked up, scarf falling in front of the shoulder, serene total concentration. Include only the plain desk edge, one sheet of paper and a small inkstone, drawn compactly so the figure remains dominant. No glyph written on the paper, blank page only.

## 5. 胜利 victory（入门完成）

- 动作说明：直立收势，右手将两张字卡叠拢收于胸前贴于心口，左手拄巨笔立于身侧作杖，下颌微抬望向右前方远处，围巾自然垂落，神情克制欣慰，无握拳欢呼。
- 提示词：

  Pose: upright composed aftermath stance facing right, both cards gathered against his chest held in the right hand close to his heart, left hand resting atop the giant brush planted vertically at his side like a staff, chin lifted gazing into the distance, scarf hanging calm, quiet hard-won relief on the face, no cheering gesture. Cards show blank backs only.
