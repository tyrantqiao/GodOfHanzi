## 1. 雪林受困（霜藤战）

- 远中近景描述：远——淡墨雪山脊没入雪灰天空；中——斜出冻松两三株与倒伏断木，墨青浓淡勾干鳞；近——画面左右缘垂霜藤须与浅雪坡起伏，中央雪面留开阔战斗区。
- 光照色板：铅云散射顶光，无明确光源点；主色雪灰与墨青，藤影用灰青 `#597b79` 压角；禁暖色（暖色留给火特效）。
- 分层建议：far=山脊与天空一笔可换；mid=松干断木（人物遮挡层）；near=缘角藤须与雪坡前沿，覆盖立绘脚底制造纵深。
- 提示词（拼接共用框架后追加）：

  Scene: a trapped clearing in a frozen pine forest. Two gnarled frost pines lean in from the mid-ground, one split fallen trunk lies half-buried, hanging icicle-whiskers of dead vine fringe the frame corners, low snow drifts ripple across the near ground, a faint circle of trampled snow at centre-left hinting someone struggled here. Oppressive stillness, cold blue-grey dusk light, the clearing itself bright enough for silhouettes.

## 2. 雪林制卡休憩处（笔案/剧情节点）

- 远中近景描述：远——雪林边缘透出一线将暮的天光；中——一处背风的岩窝与横倒巨木搭成的半棚，棚下石面平整如案；近——熄了一半的小炭炉、散放纸墨的空位（器物画小画偏，中央留出笔案 UI 空间）、垂落避风的枯草帘。
- 光照色板：全幅冷灰蓝为底，唯一暖源是炭炉余烬的赭红 `#98483c` 淡晕与天光一线旧纸 `#d7cdb9`；暖区不超过画面 15%。
- 分层建议：far 天光层可整体提亮做「剧情推进」呼吸感；mid 岩窝棚架为主视觉；near 仅草帘一角，避免与制卡面板 UI 抢位。
- 提示词：

  Scene: a sheltered rest hollow at the forest edge at dusk, a windbreak of boulders with a half-fallen giant pine laid across them as a lean-to, a flat stone table surface under the shelter, a small nearly-burnt charcoal brazier glowing faint ochre at the lower right corner, dry grass curtain hanging from one beam, gentle last daylight seeping between distant trunks. Quiet safe mood, warmth minimal and low, centre of image kept open and pale for interface overlay.

## 3. 残碑藤妖战场（残碑战）

- 远中近景描述：远——墨青色断崖列屏，崖间悬一道细雾瀑；中——荒废碑阵三四通，最高一通自中断裂、缝中透出灰青冷光，藤影爬满碑座；近——碎碑块与倾覆石兽半埋浅雪，地面霜纹向中央碑阵汇聚。
- 光照色板：无天光，整体旧纸灰调；碑缝冷光用灰青 `#597b79` 低亮度渐晕，草绿 `#667c51` 只作藤叶点色；禁金色（赭金留给高品质反馈）。
- 分层建议：far 断崖、mid 碑阵（与 A05 `broken-stele.svg` 同语言，便于前景立碑与背景碑群呼应）、near 碎碑石兽压角；敌人站位正对最高断碑，形成「碑是它的本体」误读空间。
- 提示词：

  Scene: a ruined stele field beneath a dark cliff screen, a thin mist waterfall threading the cliff gap, three or four weathered stone steles standing crooked, the tallest split down the seam with a cold grey-teal glow seeping from inside, thick ink-green vines clawing over its base, broken stele shards and a toppled stone guardian beast half-buried in the near snow, frost veins on the ground radiating toward the central stele. Sinister solemn atmosphere, ancient heritage being devoured, no readable inscription marks, all carved patterns abstract.
